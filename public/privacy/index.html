VaultGuard Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

VaultGuard is a zero-knowledge encrypted password manager. This privacy policy describes how VaultGuard handles your data. The short version: we don't collect, store, transmit, or have access to any of your data — ever.

Data Storage

  • All your vault data (passwords, usernames, notes, etc.) is stored exclusively on your device using your browser's built-in IndexedDB storage.
  • Data never leaves your device. No data is sent to any server, cloud service, or third party.
  • VaultGuard has no servers, no databases, and no backend infrastructure.

Encryption

  • All vault entries are encrypted using AES-256-GCM encryption before being stored on your device.
  • Encryption keys are derived from your master password using PBKKDF2 with 600,000 iterations.
  • Your master password is never stored — only a salted hash is kept locally to verify unlocks.
  • Even if someone gains access to your device's storage, they cannot read your passwords without your master password.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal information — we don't ask for your name, email, or any identifying information
  • No usage analytics — we don't track how you use the app
  • No cookies — not even for analytics or tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No device information — we don't collect device model, OS version, or hardware details
  • No network data — we don't monitor or log network activity
  • No location data

Third-Party Services

VaultGuard does not use any third-party services, SDKs, trackers, or analytics tools. There are no ads, no social sharing buttons, and no external content loaded from other domains.

Export & Import

  • When you export your vault as JSON, the data is re-encrypted using your master password. The exported file can only be read by someone who knows your master password.
  • When you export as CSV, passwords are saved in plain text. This is your choice and your responsibility — you are warned before exporting in this format.
  • Exported files are saved to your device. VaultGuard does not upload them anywhere.

Children's Privacy

VaultGuard does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, this policy inherently complies with children's privacy regulations (COPPA, etc.).

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored only on your device, you have full control over deletion. To delete all your data: open VaultGuard, go to Settings, and choose "Delete Vault". This permanently removes all encrypted data from your device's IndexedDB storage. There is no server-side data to delete because none exists.

Changes to This Policy

If VaultGuard's data practices ever change (for example, if a sync feature were added), this privacy policy will be updated accordingly. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can open an issue on the VaultGuard GitHub repository.
